03 · Our first moment

Naa cheyi pattukunna aa moment 💗

Station lo nannu chudagane naa cheyi pattukunnav… aa moment naaku eppatiki special.

Imagine aa moment malli… nee cheyi naa chethilo 🫶
Nee tho aa challani morning ride 🛵❤️

Oka chinna pillodu thappipothadu emo ani jagrathaga nannu pick cheskunnav. Tharvatha nee scooty meedha nee pakkana kurchoni, challani weather lo, cool ga unna morning breeze madhya travel chesthunte… aa feeling chaala peaceful ga, romantic ga undhi. ❤️

“Nee pakkana unte, aa simple ride kuda naaku oka beautiful dream la anipinchindi.” 💞
